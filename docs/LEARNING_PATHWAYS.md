# Learning pathway foundation

Four curriculum stages are sequenced in the existing browser demo: beginning sounds (CAT), digraphs (FISH), repeated letter manipulation (BANANA) and ten-letter words (STRAWBERRY). Each next stage requires the prior word to be completed **independently twice**. Hints, wrong checks, and assisted completions do not count as independent mastery. Progress and recommendation are stored locally in the learner browser only. The shared pathway rules are platform-independent and unit tested.

The pathway progress panel allows replaying unlocked stages and clearing progress. This is **not** the full adaptive curriculum or a professionally reviewed phonics progression. The four sample words and IPA are development-only; the 30–50 word starter bank, age-sensitive pathway variants, admin-published sequence, accommodations, offline content packs and educator review are later milestones. The native app is not yet wired to the pathway progress tracker.
